Example scoring convention

Starting at 100, judge each subtraction by seven relative to the preceding answer. In this scripted example 93 and 86 are correct, 78 is incorrect, and 71 follows 78 by seven. This is an example convention, not a mandatory clinical scoring rule.

What this demonstrates — and what it does not
Available online
Synthetic timeline playback; response edits and manual grading; task-window validation; live speech counts/accuracy/latency; precomputed gait references; QC and downloadable XLSX/CSV/JSON.
Full local toolkit only
Real video/audio handling, MMPose/Whisper recognition, keypoint correction, gait extraction, and leakage-controlled cohort analyses. No real recordings, trained classifier, live GPU service or manuscript-performance claim is distributed in this public demo.
Privacy: this static website makes no application API calls and has no upload facility or analytics scripts. Review edits are not transmitted. The hosting provider may log normal requests/IP addresses under its own policy. Export files may contain the demo notes you entered; inspect them before sharing.
